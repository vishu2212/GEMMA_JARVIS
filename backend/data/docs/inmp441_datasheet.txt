INMP441 OMNIDIRECTIONAL MEMS MICROPHONE WITH I2S INTERFACE DATASHEET

1. GENERAL DESCRIPTION
The INMP441 is a high performance, low power, digital output, omnidirectional MEMS microphone with bottom port. The complete INMP441 solution consists of a MEMS sensor, signal conditioning, analog-to-digital converter, anti-aliasing filter, power management and industry standard 24-bit I2S interface. The I2S interface allows the INMP441 to be directly connected to digital processors, such as DSPs and microcontrollers like ESP32, without the need for an audio codec in the system.

2. SPECIFICATIONS & AUDIO PERFORMANCE
- Interface: Industry Standard I2S (Inter-IC Sound)
- Resolution: 24-bit PCM data output
- Maximum I2S Sample Rate: 50 kHz (typically operated at 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz, or 16 kHz for speech recognition)
- Minimum I2S Sample Rate: 6.25 kHz
- Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR): 61 dBA
- Sensitivity: -26 dBFS
- Frequency Response: 60 Hz to 15 kHz
- Supply Voltage (VDD): 1.8 V to 3.3 V
- Current Consumption: 1.4 mA active mode, 10 uA sleep mode

3. PIN CONFIGURATION & HARDWARE CONNECTIONS
- VDD: Power Supply (Connect to 3.3V power rail)
- GND: Power Ground (Connect to system GND)
- SD (Serial Data): I2S Data Output (Connect to ESP32 GPIO 4)
- WS (Word Select / LRCLK): I2S Left/Right Clock (Connect to ESP32 GPIO 42)
- SCK (Serial Clock / BCLK): I2S Bit Clock (Connect to ESP32 GPIO 41)
- L/R (Left/Right Select): Selects Left or Right channel. Connect to GND for Left channel output, or VDD for Right channel output. For single-channel ESP32 speech recognition, connect L/R to GND.

4. I2S TIMING AND OPERATING MODES
The INMP441 operates as an I2S slave device. It requires external SCK and WS clock signals provided by the I2S master controller (e.g. ESP32 I2S master driver). Data is output on the falling edge of SCK and sampled by the master processor on the rising edge of SCK.
